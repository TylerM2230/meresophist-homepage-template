mere sophist

A public record of learning — philosophy, mathematics, literature, and the crafts that live in the rooms between them. Kept on shelves so the archive has a spine, and read slowly so the reading has a pulse, and made in the open and revised without shame. Nothing here pretends to be finished; everything here is willing to be turned over in your hands.
